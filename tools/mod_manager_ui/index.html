Set up your camera

Three steps. Everything this app changes can be undone with one click.

1Connect your camera
  1. Charge the camera battery (at least half full).
  2. On the camera: MENU → Setup → USB Connection → Auto.
  3. On the camera: turn Airplane Mode OFF.
  4. Turn the camera on and plug it into this PC with the USB cable.
Looking for your camera...
Camera not found?
  • Use the cable that came with the camera. Some cables only charge and cannot carry data.
  • Plug straight into the PC, not into a USB hub or keyboard. Try another USB port.
  • Close other camera apps (Imaging Edge, Lightroom import, phone sync tools).
  • Unplug the cable, wait 5 seconds, and plug it back in. This app keeps looking by itself.

Before we start

Camera USB Driver (Service Mode)

PMCA tweaks, the debugger bootstrap, and onboarding use Sony service mode. Windows must bind WinUSB to the camera's mass-storage device and to its service-mode device (054C:0336). This replaces the manual Zadig step. Each install asks for administrator approval and writes a receipt. While WinUSB is bound, the camera no longer appears as a drive in Explorer (use a card reader for SD Card Sync).

Set the camera's USB Connection to Auto, not permanent Mass Storage.

Installing... don't unplug the camera

Now restart the camera (first time only)
  1. Turn the camera OFF and unplug the USB cable.
  2. Make sure Airplane Mode is OFF.
  3. Wait 3 seconds, turn the camera ON, and plug the cable back in.

This window continues by itself when the camera is back.
